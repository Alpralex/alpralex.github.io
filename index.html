Where a 10-store retailer loses sales

Five years of real daily sales from 10 Walmart stores in the US. The data is public: Walmart released it as the M5 data set for a forecasting competition. I built the data model and dashboard to show where the stores lose sales and how good the competition's best forecast really is.

  • Empty shelves cost about $0.79M a year, and 80% of that is in food
  • The forecast is accurate per store (5% error) but not per product (75%), so stock planning needs a safety margin
  • 47.7 million rows of sales, with automated checks on the data

Tools: SQL, dbt, DuckDB, Python, Power BI, DAX

Budget vs actual for a group in four countries

The monthly report a finance team uses to explain results: actual figures against budget and forecast, for a fictional distributor with companies in Portugal, Spain, France and the UK. The data is synthetic and built with known stories, so anyone can check that the report finds them.

  • Shows that the group beats its budget only because Portugal raised prices; without that, profit would be 15% below plan
  • Separates the effect of the weaker pound (€0.36M of UK sales) from the business's real performance
  • Each country controller sees only the figures of their own company

Tools: SQL, dbt, DuckDB, Python, Power BI, DAX, row-level security, Qlik Sense

Where Portugal's public procurement money goes

Every public contract published in Portugal from 2019 to 2026: 1.55 million contracts worth €120 billion, from the government's open data. The report shows who buys, what, from whom, and how much competition there is.

  • Contracts awarded without a tender are half of all contracts but only 17% of the money
  • Far more contracts sit just below the legal limits for awarding without a tender than just above them
  • Real open data, cleaned and checked; private individuals are never named

Tools: SQL, dbt, DuckDB, Python, Power BI, DAX, Qlik Sense

InfoCars: used-car prices for car dealers

A product I built and sold through my company, Informar Dados. Every day it collected used-car ads, recognised the model of each car and showed the dealer the market price, so they could buy and price cars with confidence. The dashboard here runs on sample data.

  • About 250,000 car ads in the database
  • 5,114 different spellings of car names reduced to 641 real models
  • About €300 saved per negotiation at the dealer that bought it

Tools: SQL, PostgreSQL, Python, dbt, Power BI, Qlik Sense, Claude API

Sales director dashboard for a distributor

The app a sales director opens every morning: sales, sales reps' visits, discounts and late payments, for an animal-health distributor in Portugal. The data is synthetic, modelled on the kind of app I relied on as a commercial director.

Is housing in Portugal getting less affordable?

House prices, rents and incomes in Portugal compared with six other EU countries, from official Eurostat data.

  • Compared with incomes, homes cost 36% more than in 2015; across the EU, 6% more
